Privacy & Cookies

How Hyaluronic.bio handles analytics, cookies and external links.

1. Who we are

Hyaluronic.bio is an information-only website operated by ronarn AS, Norway. The site explains hyaluronic acid and hydration in plain language.

2. What data we collect

We do not collect names, addresses or account details. We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage data such as:

  • Which pages are visited and how often
  • Approximate country and device type
  • Referring websites (where visitors came from)

IP addresses are processed by Google in an anonymised form. We do not attempt to identify individual visitors.

3. Cookies

Hyaluronic.bio itself uses minimal cookies related to analytics. When you click through to Hylaronic.com or retailers such as Amazon, those sites may set their own cookies.

4. External links & affiliate programs

Hyaluronic.bio links to Hylaronic.com, where some product links may be affiliate links. If you purchase through those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Hyaluronic.bio itself does not contain affiliate links.
